# Checks in a headset

The owner plays in the headset and checks only what a person must see, hear
or feel: the list "What the owner checks", in the order that a normal play
meets it. The agents do the rest. The log of a normal play tells the faults
in "Play as normal and send the log", and desktop runs prove the other checks
(`roadmap.md`, R12).

## What the owner checks

Each check says what to look at and what is wrong. A check that names a
switch decides a default: try the switch one time, and say which value you
want.

### The start

1. **The stories.** Watch the first story of a new game in the window. Then
   play one story with the launcher row "Cut scenes" at "Around you". Wrong:
   a screen or subtitles at a bad place or size, a close shot or a cut that
   hurts the eyes, an advisor that is too low or moves with the head, or a
   story around you that makes you sick. Point at the screen in the window.
   Wrong: hands or a ray that are not at your real hands, or a ray that is
   hard to see on the scene.

### The God view

2. **The size of the world.** Look at the land at your waist at each scale,
   and bring the Hand near your eyes. Wrong: a world that feels too large or
   too small, land or clouds that look wrong at a scale, or a Hand that the
   view cuts near the eyes. Then bend down slowly and put your head into a
   hill, and then into a house. Wrong: you see under the land or into the
   house, the gold and red cover comes too late or too soon, it flickers on
   a slope, or its color is hard on the eyes. At the nearest zoom, with the
   land pulled down to your feet, a villager must look as high as you.
   Wrong: land or villagers that flicker or look wrong there. Look across
   the island from a hill there. Wrong: far trees and houses that pop in or
   out, or fog that hides the far land.
3. **The turn and the comfort vignette.** Set the settings row "Turn" to
   Smooth, and turn with a small push and a full push. Wrong: discomfort,
   or a speed or a curve of the push that feels wrong. Then set the
   launcher row "Comfort vignette" to
   gentle and to strong for a short walk. Wrong: it does not help, or it is
   in the way. Then set "Movement type" to Fly and fly down to the land and
   up, with the head and with the controller, and hold a full push. Wrong:
   discomfort, a fly that does not go where you look or point, or a speed
   ramp that is too slow or too fast.
4. **The teleport.** Aim the arc at near and far land, and go to the village
   of Land 1. Wrong: an arc or a ring that is hard to see or to aim, or a way
   to the village that you cannot find.

### The Hand

5. **The hands on the controllers.** Look at each hand, and close the
   trigger and the grip slowly. Wrong: a hand off its controller, turned or
   of a wrong size, a finger that jumps, or an object that looks wrong in the
   palm (name the object). Touch the tips of the two real controllers.
   Wrong: drawn tips that do not touch (the fit of `controller-hints.md`,
   9.15). Reach slowly to the edge of your influence and
   past it, with each hand. Wrong: a red glow at the edge that comes too
   late or too early, that is hard to see or too strong, or a fade of the
   hand that is too strong.
6. **The grip.** Take things, set them down and let them go. Wrong: a take
   from a loose hand, a grip that lets go late or not at all, a drop by
   accident, or a job that needs the other button.
7. **The aim and the halo.** Compare the ray with the ray of the Quest menu.
   Take near and far things: a villager among trees, a tree beside the
   Creature, a thing past a near tree. Wrong: rays that differ, a halo on a
   thing that you did not aim at, a halo or a glow that is lost on the land
   or too strong, or a pull by accident. The glow of the ray, the tether and
   the arc is the whole line. Wrong: a glow that is hard to see on the land
   or the sky.
8. **The fingers on the world.** Put a hand on the land, at a wall of a
    house, on a villager and into the sea, and take a small rock. Push a
    hand deep into the land, into a wall of a house and into a tree trunk,
    and move it there. Wrong: a finger that jumps,
    shakes, bends in the air or goes into the thing, a claw on a small rock,
    fingers that stop on the water, a hand that goes into the land or a wall,
    or jumps on it, or a hand on the land or a wall that feels wrong beside
    the real hand.
9. **The haptics.** Feel the pulses of a take, a release, a stroke, the
    leash, a command and the land. Wrong: a pulse that is too weak or too
    strong, or a pattern that feels like a buzz or hides the pulse of a
    control.
10. **Throws.** Throw rocks, villagers, trees and a Fireball at
    each scale. Wrong: a throw that goes too short, too far or in a wrong
    direction, a quick set down that throws, or a flight or a landing that
    looks wrong.
### The village

11. **The words over the land.** Wrong: the job icon (2 times) or the name of a villager (150 percent) is hard to read at a glance.
12. **Small targets.** Tap the icons of a Worship Site, reach for a scroll,
    move the totem pole, and tap a leash post at the door of the Temple.
    Wrong: a tap that hits the item beside the one that you want, a pole that
    slides from the hand, a count of the totem that is hard to hit, or a
    second hand on the pole.
13. **The building of a scaffold.** Hold a scaffold over the land and roll
    the wrist. Wrong: the building turns the other way than the wrist, turns
    when the hand only moves, or a full turn of the building is hard to
    make.

### The Creature

14. **Stroke, slap and look.** Stroke the Creature slowly at each zoom, slap
    it with the arm and with the wrist, and lean while it looks at you. Also
    rest the open hand in its body and move it slowly, and reach past it to
    a thing near it. Wrong: a slap that does not count, a stroke that counts
    as a slap, a touch in the air, a look past your head, a stroke when the
    hand only goes past, or a Creature panel that goes on and off.
15. **Leash and commands.** With the leash on, tap the trigger at
    places and things, give it an object, and shake the leash hand. Wrong: the
    Creature goes to a different place, a command or a change of the leash
    by accident, or a real
    shake that does nothing. Also wrong: a green ring on the land that is
    hard to see or that you take for a target of the grip, or a glow on a
    thing that the grip does not take.

### Miracles

16. **The spell book and the row of gestures.** Open the book (X, or the
    palm up), pull a miracle out, and let one spring back. Hold B and look at
    the row over the palm. Wrong: a book or a row that is hard to read or to
    reach, a pull that feels stiff or loose, a book that opens by accident,
    a bubble that looks wrong, or an orb in a cell that is not in its hole
    of the page or is hard to see over the land behind it. The pointing hand
    must show in front of the page when it is in front, with no edge of
    page over it or band of land beside it.
17. **A gesture and a cast.** Draw miracles in the air, draw a circle for
    Storm or a shield, and try the switch "Gesture point" (debug page,
    Miracles). Take a miracle out with a shake and with a tap of B. Wrong: a
    draw that looks right and does not match (say which), a circle that gets
    a different size on the land, or a way out that acts by accident or not
    at all.
18. **Water on a fire.** Set a house on fire with a Fireball, and pour Water
    on it. Wrong: the fire does not go out, the pour looks wrong, a second
    hand, or a red flow on the hand that looks wrong or is not on your hand.

### The Temple

19. **The rooms of the Temple.** Come in, turn, tilt and lean the head, go
    through doors, point at things, and look down at the pool of the hall.
    Wrong: a room that turns or tilts with the head, a room of a wrong size,
    one dark eye, a pace that is too fast or too slow, a move at the pool, a
    door that a tilted head cannot open, or a second hand in the room. Save
    a game and look at its picture on the wall. Wrong: a picture that looks
    blocky or rough at the edges.

### Menus and cards

20. **The menus, the keyboard, the ring and its sheets.** Open the game menu
    and the ring, type the name of a god, add a bookmark from the sheet of
    the places with the ray and the grip, go to it, choose a leash on the
    sheet of the leashes, and turn the head while a menu is open. Wrong: an
    icon or a word of the ring that you cannot read at a glance, or a tile of
    a sheet that is hard to put the hand on. Wrong: a panel that turns with the head or is at a bad
    place, a panel that hides the pointing hand, keys, buttons or a ring that
    are blurred or hard to read or hard to hit with the ray, a ray that does
    not meet the arrow of the game, or a thumb that lets go of Y. Wrong
    also: a ray that a panel or the welcome page hides, or a glow at the end
    of the ray that is not round, has an outline, is hard to see on the paper
    or on the land, or is not at the depth of the panel. With
    `debug_menu=1`, also open the debug window, read a note, and scroll a
    long group with the stick. Wrong: rows or notes hard to read, arrows hard
    to hit, or a scroll that is too fast or too slow. Open the VR
    Settings window from page 2 of the ring. Wrong: a label or a value that
    is hard to read, text that shimmers when the head moves, or models on the tab Controls that do not look like
    your controllers, with leaders or boxes that are hard to follow.
21. **The cards and the subtitles.** Read the hint cards at the wrist and the
    subtitle card while the hands work. Wrong: low contrast or a shimmer, a
    faint line around a card, a card that covers a hand or often changes side, a prompt that is late or
    stays too long, a subtitle card that moves while the head moves, a
    save of the game with no "Saving..." card in view for the whole save,
    or a picture of a button beside its word that is hard to read or does
    not show where the button is on your controller. The controller in the
    hand (`hint_style=2`): wrong is a model that is not at your real
    controller, a lit button that is not the one to press, or a card whose
    line you cannot follow to its button.

### All through the play

22. **The picture in both eyes.** Look for a fault in one eye only: a faint
    old picture, a flash of lightning, a flicker or a black frame. Also look
    at the edge of the view in a fast turn of the head, and turn the head at
    the load of a saved game. Wrong: a fault in one eye, black at the edge,
    or a picture that does not turn with the head at a load.
23. **The sound.** Listen while you play. Wrong: a Creature that sounds at
    the height of your ears and not below, a sea that turns with the view, a
    sound hard in one ear, a sound that lags a fast turn, a click or a cut, a
    loop that stops, or a cue that is too loud.
24. **The view for videos** (`video_view=1`). Record a short video of the
    game window with OBS while you play. Wrong: corner marks that you
    cannot see or that disturb the play, a video that shakes, a video that
    cuts off what you look at, or a card, the ring or a panel that is not
    in the video or not where you saw it.

## Play as normal and send the log

The mod writes `bwvr.log` beside `runblack.exe`. After a play, send it and
each `bwvr-old-*.log` of that day. When an item needs a key, an agent sets
the key in `bwvr.ini` before the play. "Needs a log line" is work for the
agents: the log does not tell the item yet.

- **A crash** (B38). Log: `address space:`, and the last lines before the
  stop.
- **White or invisible story people with Vulkan** (B38): the Lost Brother and
  the Lost Sister of Land 1, and Sable at the cow stone. Log: `vk scene     :
  a texture with no new image`.
- **A save in the Save Game Room** of a game in Program Files with no
  administrator rights. Log: `save store   :` gives each call that the mod
  made again on the VirtualStore copy.
- **A take at the Hand that stops when the hand moves away** (P21). Log:
  `grip         : the grip falls fast during a bulk take and stays closed`
  names the cause that `near_take_keeps=1` holds, with the grip values of
  the last 12 frames. A take that still stops has `grip         : the grip
  opens ... Its values over the last 12 frames` and `the grip opens during a
  bulk take` (the real grip opened), or `hand bulk    : the bulk take ends`
  with the button of the game down (the game ended it), with the distance
  from the pile and the influence.
- **A double picture in play on a headset with canted views** (Pimax, N17).
  Log: `eye cant     :` gives the turn of each view from the head, and if
  each eye pass turns its camera by it (`eye_cant=1`).
- **An old picture in one eye** (B16). Log: `eye clean    : an eye pass drew
  no sky` names the scene of that frame.
- **A miracle that shows nothing** (Storm, a shield, Shrink, Enlarge,
  Invisibility). Log: `miracle watch:` says if the game made the spell, its
  radius in cm of the room and how long it stands, or what a Creature
  miracle wrote on the Creature and its size. The start of a pour, for

- **Far trees, houses or villagers that are not drawn** with a long model

  no such line is not a light on the land.

- **The same picture with each part of the session with Vulkan**

- **Slow frames and stutter** (N1). Log: `worst frame  :`, `runtime calls:`

- **The pace of the runtime at 120 Hz** (N1). Log: `display      : the

## The miracles of the game

This section says what each miracle does in the plain game, so that a
player and a desktop run can compare the mod with it. The owner checks one
miracle (check 21, Water on a fire). Each line "To prove" is for a desktop
run (`roadmap.md`, R12): the effect is in the game state or in the eye
pictures.

**The sources.** The game's own text (the help text, the tooltips, the tips
of the loading screen) is in `Scripts\InfoScript2.txt` of the game folder.
The costs and the gestures are in `Scripts\info.dat`. The words here are a
short summary of that text. Where the game files do not say a thing, this
section says "not confirmed".

**Prayer power.** Each miracle costs prayer power from the Worship Site.
The worshippers of the village make it. The costs below are the cost field
of `info.dat`. Desktop runs confirm it for Water (5000), Food (7000), Heal
(6000) and Forest (13000).

**The gestures.** A miracle of the god starts with the spiral. A Creature
miracle starts with the inverse spiral. Then comes the gesture of the
miracle. The names of the shapes below come from the order of the game's
list of gesture types. The spell book and the row over the palm show the
real picture of each one.

**No shower.** The game has no shower or mega version of a miracle. A
stronger version is a power up: draw its gesture (the spiral or the inverse
spiral) while the miracle is in the Hand. Only a miracle from a Worship Site
can power up. The announcer calls the steps "Increase", "Extreme" and
"Maximus". "Shower of Life" is only the name of the Water miracle.

**How to get a miracle in the mod** (`miracles.md`, `village.md`):

1. The spell book: press X (or turn the palm of that hand up), put the
   pointing hand on a miracle, close the grip, and pull 10 cm.
2. A gesture in the air: hold B and draw the spiral and then the gesture of
   the miracle.
3. The Worship Site: tap the grip on the icon of the miracle.
4. A one-off bubble on the land: a short grip on it makes it active. Or
   carry it, and press the trigger.

**How to cast it in the mod** (`miracles.md`, "The grip casts"):

| Kind | Miracles | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At a place | Heal, Forest, Teleport, Explosion | Close the grip with the Hand over the place |
| A pour | Water, Food, Wood, Lightning | Keep the grip closed over the place. Open it to stop |
| A throw | Fireball, the two flocks | Close the grip, swing the hand, and open it |
| A circle | Storm, the two shields | Close the grip and draw a circle over the land. The cast comes when the circle closes |
| On a Creature | The Creature miracles | Close the grip with the gold halo on a Creature (at the Hand, or in the aim) |

Take a miracle out: tap B, or shake the hand. A miracle from a Worship Site
gives its prayer power back. A one-off miracle that you take out is lost.

**What the desktop runs show now.** A desktop run shows that the game gets
the cast (the log line `the game casts ...`). The eye pictures also show the
effect of Food, Forest, Explosion, Freeze, Enlarge and Shrink, a Fireball on
a house, Water on burning trees and the dome of a Physical Shield. The other
effects in the world are not proven yet.

**Which miracles you have.** The towns of each land give their miracles.
The debug switch "Unlock all" (debug page, the group Miracles) gives each
town all miracles with no cost. But a Worship Site shows only the first 6 that get a
slot, for example Storm, Forest, Fireball, Food and the two shields. Then
Water and Heal do not show.

### Water ("Shower of Life")

- **Plain game.** It makes a flow of water where the Hand is, for as long as
  you hold the cast. The game's tips give three uses:
  - It puts out a fire.
  - It waters a field, so that the crops grow faster.
  - It waters a forest, so that the forest grows larger and gives more wood.
  
  The Creature can learn it and water fields, trees and fires. Not
  confirmed: what it does to a villager or to the Creature (no game file says
  it does anything).
- **How to see that it worked.** The fire test is the clear one: set a house
  on fire with a Fireball, and pour Water on it. Each drop lowers the heat
  of the house by a small part, so a house needs a long pour, and it can
  catch fire again from a fire near it. The
  growth of a field or a forest is slow, and the game gives no message for
  it. Not confirmed: how fast the growth is, and whether a field shows a
  change at once.
- **Cost.** 5000. The power up "Water Miracle Increase" (spiral): 7000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the W shape. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the place, and open it to stop.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour, the request from the spell book, and
  a power up from a Worship Site. A pour of 27 casts puts out burning trees
  (`miracle_run.ps1 -Kind fire`). A pour on a burning house puts it out:
  the drops take it under the temperature at which it burns in 4 to 7
  drops, and no flames stay on it. A pile of wood and trees beside it, which
  the pour does not reach, burn on (`miracles.md`, "Water on a fire").
- **The owner's check.** Check 21 of "What the owner checks".
- **To prove.** Water on a field and on a forest changes their growth.

### Food ("Horn of Plenty")

- **Plain game.** It makes food, which drops on the land under the Hand while
  you hold the cast. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** Food lies on the land under the Hand. Not
  confirmed: the look of the pile, and whether the villagers carry it to the
  Village Store by themselves.
- **Cost.** 7000. The power up (spiral): 10000. Not confirmed: what the power
  up makes. One tooltip calls it "Food Miracle Extreme". The help text and
  another tooltip call it "Poisoned Food", food that makes people sick.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the S shape. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the place.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour, the request from the spell book, a
  power up, and food that falls and lies on the land under the Hand.
- **To prove.** Food with a power up: say what it makes (more food, or food
  that makes people sick).

### Wood ("Structure Elemental")

- **Plain game.** It makes wood on the land under the Hand while you hold the
  cast. The villagers use wood to build. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** Wood lies on the land, and you can take it
  to the Village Store or to a scaffold.
- **Cost.** 7000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the three. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the place.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour.
- **To prove.** Wood poured on the land goes to the Village Store with the
  Hand.

### Forest ("Seeds of Existence")

- **Plain game.** It puts a forest at the place that you choose. The trees
  give wood to the foresters. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** New trees stand at the place.
- **Cost.** 13000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the Cyrillic L. At a place.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip with the Hand over the place. The game has
  a camera path that turns around the new forest. The mod starts no path, so
  the camera stays (`miracle_camera_still=1`).
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the cast at a place. Young trees stand at the
  place under the Hand 8 s after the cast and grow taller, and the view does
  not turn.

### Heal ("Wellness")

- **Plain game.** It heals people. The power up heals all the people of an
  area. The plague quest of the game uses it. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** A sick or hurt villager recovers. In the
  plague quest the good spirit says when you heal one. Not confirmed: a
  picture that shows the heal on a villager.
- **Cost.** 6000. The power up (spiral): 9000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the heart. At a place: the game casts it at a
  place on the land, not on one villager.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip with the Hand over the people.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the cast at a place, and the request from the
  spell book.
- **To prove.** Sick villagers under the Hand recover (for example in the
  plague quest). A test save of Land 1 has no sick villager, so the check is
  in "Play as normal and send the log".

### Fireball ("Sphere Aflame")

- **Plain game.** A ball of fire that you throw. The first power up is a
  multi-Fireball, and the second is the largest Fireball. The Creature can
  learn it. The help text says only how to throw it. That it sets the place
  where it lands on fire is known from play, not from a game file.
- **How to see that it worked.** The ball flies from the hand, and the place
  where it lands burns.
- **Cost.** 3500. The power ups (inverse spiral, then spiral): 7000 and
  10000.

### Lightning ("Forked Fire")

- **Plain game.** A bolt of lightning at the target, for as long as you hold
  the cast. The power ups give a larger, more forked bolt. The Creature can
  learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** The bolt starts at the tip of the index
  finger and forks to objects and to the land near the Hand, in the
  direction in which the camera of the game looks. Near the Temple it goes
  to the spire. The land goes white in both eyes (B18). The thin red column
  over the Temple is the beam of the Temple heart of the game, in the color
  of the player. Not confirmed: what the bolt does to each kind of target.
- **Cost.** 5000. The power ups (inverse spiral, then spiral): 7500 and
  10000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the target.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour, and the start of the bolt at the
  Hand's point (`the spell ... gets its gesture place from the game`, 0.0
  cm).
- **To prove.** The bolt starts at the tip of the index finger in both
  eyes, with the Hand in the air.

### Storm ("Storm of Ages")

- **Plain game.** A storm over a circle that you draw. The plain storm is
  wind. The first power up adds rain, and the second adds rain and
  lightning. A storm puts out fires. The game also has a help text for a
  tornado. The Creature can learn the wind, the rain storm, the lightning
  storm and the tornado. Not confirmed: how a player makes a tornado, what
  damage a storm does, and whether its rain waters crops.
- **How to see that it worked.** Wind, then rain and lightning with the power
  ups, over the circle. A fire in the circle goes out.
- **Cost.** 8000. The power ups (inverse spiral, then spiral): 15000 and
  20000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. It needs its own circle.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip and draw a circle over the land. The cast
  comes when the game matches the circle.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the circle, the cast, and the storm under the
  hand (`miracle_circle_hand`): a grey cloud in front of the Hand, and a
  dark sky.
- **The owner's choice.** The look of the clouds over and near the head
  (B43): round clouds that clear near the eye (the default), or the flat
  clouds of the game that clear near the eye (`cloud_round_near=0`).

### Spiritual Shield ("Sanctuary")

- **Plain game.** A shield over a circle that you draw. Miracles cannot go
  in, but solid objects pass through. The shield takes damage when it is
  attacked, and it uses prayer power for as long as it stands. People under
  a shield cannot be impressed. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** A dome stands over the circle. A miracle
  cast at it does not get in.
- **Cost.** 7000, and more for each second that it stands. No power up in
  `info.dat`, although a help text names a larger shield.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. It needs its own circle.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip and draw a circle over the land.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the circle, the cast, and a dome at the
  fingertips (`miracle_circle_hand`) that stands for more than 12 s.
- **To prove.** A miracle cast at the dome does not get in.

### Physical Shield ("Armor of Faith")

- **Plain game.** A shield over a circle that you draw. It stops rocks and
  other solid objects, also those that you throw, but miracles go through.
  A thrown object bounces off. Each hit of an object that breaks houses (a
  rock) takes strength from the shield
  (`PhysicalShield::ReactToPhysicsImpact`), and a pig takes none. When the
  strength is gone, the shield breaks. The town marks the thrower as an
  attacker. It uses prayer power for as long as it stands. The Creature can
  learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** A dome stands over the circle. A rock that
  you throw at it stops.
- **Cost.** 7000, and more for each second that it stands. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. It needs its own circle.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip and draw a circle over the land.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the refusal with no circle ("needs a circle on
  the land first"), and the cast: the dome stands over the circle in the eye
  pictures (`miracle_run.ps1 -Kind pshield`). A pig thrown at the dome
  touches the shield and comes to rest on the land outside it, 19 units from
  its middle (`throw_shield=1`, `throw.md`, "A Physical Shield on the arc").
  With `throw_shield=0` it comes to rest inside.
- **To prove.** A rock thrown at the dome stops.

### Teleport ("Instant Journey")

- **Plain game.** Each cast puts one teleport site on the land, so two casts
  make two sites. A villager or the Creature that goes into a site comes out
  at the other site that is nearest its destination. Villagers on the way
  to worship use the sites when that is shorter. You can pick up a site and
  move it, and a shake in the Hand cancels it. The sites use prayer power
  until you cancel them. A tip of the game says to put them at the
  Village Store and the Worship Site, a forest or the fields. The Creature
  can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** Two sites stand on the land, and villagers
  go in at one and come out at the other.
- **Cost.** 5000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the house. At a place, two times.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip with the Hand over the place. Cast it again
  for the second site. The real hand can take a site, and a villager in the
  Hand goes onto a site when the grip opens over it
  (`miracle_site_pick=1`, `miracles.md`).
- **Proved.** Desktop runs (`miracle_run.ps1 -Kind teleport`): two casts of
  one-off seeds make two sites before the door of the Temple, about 35 units
  apart. A villager that the Hand puts on the first site comes out at the
  second one. At the Village Store the game refuses the cast.
- **To prove.** A villager on the way to worship uses the sites by himself.

### The Creature miracles

Each one acts on the target Creature, your own or a different one, for a
time. The narrator says when it "has worn off". Not confirmed: how long each
one lasts. Each is a cast on a Creature: close the grip with the gold halo
on the Creature. The gesture is the inverse spiral and then the shape below.

| Miracle | What it does to the target Creature | Cost | Gesture after the inverse spiral |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Freeze | It freezes it | 8000 | The star |
| Shrink | It makes it small | 6000 | A fork |
| Enlarge | It makes it large | 7000 | A fork |
| Weaken | It makes it weak | 8000 | A fork |
| Strengthen | It makes it strong | 8000 | A fork |
| Invisibility | It makes it invisible | 9000 | The infinity |
| Compassion ("Loving") | It makes it friendly and loving | 6000 | The heart |
| Anger ("Aggressive") | It makes it furious | 6000 | The vertical line |
| Itchy ("Holy Flies") | It irritates it | 8000 | The reverse S |

- **Proved.** Desktop runs: Strengthen and Freeze on a Creature. After
  Freeze the Creature is blue and stands still for at least 11 s, and it
  has its usual color 14 s after the cast. Shrink,
  Enlarge and Invisibility put their entry on the Creature, and Shrink and
  Enlarge change its base size (`miracle watch:`). At the door of the
  Temple the game draws the body at the size 0.22 with each spell
  (`miracles.md`, "What a cast makes"). Away from the pen the drawn body
  goes to 2.0 with Enlarge and to 0.2 with Shrink, and the eye pictures show
  the change (`miracle_run.ps1 -Kind away`). Invisibility makes the Creature
  fizz to sparkles and a shadow. Not run: the other four (N6).
- **To prove.** The narrator says that Freeze has worn off (no subtitle
  line of the log has text). Weaken, Strengthen, Compassion, Anger and Itchy
  each change the Creature as the table says. A Creature miracle at a Creature outside the influence has a
  red halo, and the card says why.

**Miracles that a player does not cast.** The game has seven more Creature
miracles: Fat, Thin, Hungry, Frightened, Tired, Ill and Thirsty. Each has a
record of a seed and a line "Your Creature has learned how to cast ...".
They have no gesture, and their records of magic have the name NONE. So a
player cannot ask for one. Not confirmed: whether a Creature or a script can
cast one in v1.42.

**The Creature casts miracles too.** The Creature can learn each miracle of
the god above, and the game says when it learns one. A tip of the game says
that a Creature casts a miracle at once when you give it the miracle.

- **To prove.** A one-off miracle given to the Creature: the Creature casts
  it. Not run in the mod.
