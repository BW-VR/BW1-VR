# Checks in a headset

The owner plays in the headset and checks only what a person must see, hear
or feel: the list "What the owner checks", in the order that a normal play
meets it. The agents do the rest. The log of a normal play tells the faults
in "Play as normal and send the log", and desktop runs prove the other checks
(`roadmap.md`, R12).

## What the owner checks

Each check says what to look at and what is wrong. A check that names a
switch decides a default: try the switch one time, and say which value you
want.

### The start

1. **The intro in stereo.** Turn on the launcher row "Intro in stereo", and
   watch the Lionhead logo. Wrong: an orb that shows double or large in one
   eye, a logo out of the reach of the arms, a motion that is not
   comfortable, or orbs that do not move with the hands.
2. **The stories.** Watch the first story of a new game in the window. Then
   play one story with the launcher row "Cut scenes" at "Around you". Wrong:
   a screen or subtitles at a bad place or size, a close shot or a cut that
   hurts the eyes, an advisor that is too low or moves with the head, or a
   story around you that makes you sick.

### The God view

3. **The size of the world.** Look at the land at your waist at each scale,
   and bring the Hand near your eyes. Wrong: a world that feels too large or
   too small, land or clouds that look wrong at a scale, or a Hand that the
   view cuts near the eyes.
4. **The zoom.** Zoom in and out with the stick. Then try the switch "Smooth
   zoom" (debug page, View). Wrong: discomfort, a blink, or a rate that is
   too fast or too slow.
5. **The walk and the grab of the land.** Walk with the stick near and far
   and on hills, and grab the land with one grip and with two grips. Wrong:
   discomfort, a wrong speed, a view that bobs or stops and jumps, land that
   shakes while the hand is still, or a bump at the edge of the Creature
   select that is too loud. Then set the launcher row "Comfort vignette" to
   gentle and to strong for a short walk. Wrong: it does not help, or it is
   in the way.
6. **The teleport.** Aim the arc at near and far land, and go to the village
   of Land 1. Wrong: an arc or a ring that is hard to see or to aim, or a way
   to the village that you cannot find.

### The Hand

7. **The hands on the controllers.** Look at each hand, and close the
   trigger and the grip slowly. Wrong: a hand off its controller, turned or
   of a wrong size, a finger that jumps, or an object that looks wrong in the
   palm (name the object).
8. **The grip.** Take things, set them down and let them go. Wrong: a take
   from a loose hand, a grip that lets go late or not at all, a drop by
   accident, or a job that needs the other button.
9. **The aim and the halo.** Compare the ray with the ray of the Quest menu.
   Take near and far things: a villager among trees, a tree beside the
   Creature, a thing past a near tree. Wrong: rays that differ, a halo on a
   thing that you did not aim at, a halo or a glow that is lost on the land
   or too strong, or a pull by accident.
10. **The fingers on the world.** Put a hand on the land, at a wall of a
    house, on a villager and into the sea, and take a small rock. Wrong: a
    finger that jumps, shakes, bends in the air or goes into the thing, a
    claw on a small rock, or fingers that stop on the water.
11. **The haptics.** Feel the pulses of a take, a release, a stroke, the
    leash, a command and the land. Wrong: a pulse that is too weak or too
    strong, or a pattern that feels like a buzz or hides the pulse of a
    control.
12. **Throws and catches.** Throw rocks, villagers, trees and a Fireball at
    each scale, and catch a rock in its fall. Wrong: a throw that goes too
    short, too far or in a wrong direction, a quick set down that throws, a
    flight or a landing that looks wrong, or a catch that feels late.
13. **A take of many.** Take wood or food from a store and from a forest, at
    the Hand and from a distance, and take a tree from a distance. Wrong: a
    stream or a count that is hard to read in depth, a pace that feels wrong,
    or a pile of the far tree that is not the pit that you mean.

### The village

14. **The words over the land.** Hold a villager and read his job icon. Read
    the name of a villager, a signpost, the yellow note over the Creature and
    a tooltip at the Hand's point. Wrong: a text that is too small, hard to
    read, double, or not at the depth of its thing. Say if the job icon is a
    problem: F18 waits for that answer.
15. **Small targets.** Tap the icons of a Worship Site, reach for a scroll,
    move the totem pole, and tap a leash post at the door of the Temple.
    Wrong: a tap that hits the item beside the one that you want, a pole that
    slides from the hand, or a count of the totem that is hard to hit.

### The Creature

16. **Stroke, slap and look.** Stroke the Creature slowly at each zoom, slap
    it with the arm and with the wrist, and lean while it looks at you.
    Wrong: a slap that does not count, a stroke that counts as a slap, a
    touch in the air, or a look past your head.
17. **Leash, commands and the stay.** With the leash on, tap the trigger at
    places and things. Hold the grip on the Creature for a stay, give it an
    object, and shake the leash hand. Wrong: the Creature goes to a different
    place, a command, a stay or a change of the leash by accident, or a real
    shake that does nothing.

### Miracles

18. **The spell book and the row of gestures.** Open the book (X, or the
    palm up), pull a miracle out, and let one spring back. Hold B and look at
    the row over the palm. Wrong: a book or a row that is hard to read or to
    reach, a pull that feels stiff or loose, a book that opens by accident,
    or a bubble that looks wrong.
19. **A gesture and a cast.** Draw miracles in the air, draw a circle for
    Storm or a shield, and try the switch "Gesture point" (debug page,
    Creature). Take a miracle out with a shake and with a tap of B. Wrong: a
    draw that looks right and does not match (say which), a circle that gets
    a different size on the land, or a way out that acts by accident or not
    at all.
20. **Water on a fire.** Set a house on fire with a Fireball, and pour Water
    on it. Wrong: the fire does not go out, or the pour looks wrong.

### The Temple

21. **The rooms of the Temple.** Come in, turn and lean the head, go through
    doors, point at things, and look down at the pool of the hall. Wrong: a
    room that turns or tilts with the head, a room of a wrong size, one dark
    eye, a pace that is too fast or too slow, or a move at the pool.

### Menus and cards

22. **The menus, the keyboard, the ring and the panel of places.** Open the
    game menu and the ring, type the name of a god, open the panel of places
    from page 2 of the ring and go to a bookmark, and turn the head while a
    menu is open. Wrong: a panel that turns with the head or is at a bad
    place, a panel that hides the pointing hand, keys, buttons or a ring that
    are blurred or hard to read or hard to hit with the ray, a ray that does
    not meet the arrow of the game, or a thumb that lets go of Y.
23. **The cards and the subtitles.** Read the hint cards at the wrist and the
    subtitle card while the hands work. Wrong: low contrast or a shimmer, a
    card that covers a hand or often changes side, a prompt that is late or
    stays too long, a subtitle card that moves while the head moves, or a
    save of the game with no "Saving..." card in view for the whole save.

### All through the play

24. **The picture in both eyes.** Look for a fault in one eye only: a faint
    old picture, a flash of lightning, a flicker or a black frame. Also look
    at the edge of the view in a fast turn of the head, and turn the head at
    the load of a saved game. Wrong: a fault in one eye, black at the edge,
    or a picture that does not turn with the head at a load.
25. **The sound.** Listen while you play. Wrong: a Creature that sounds at
    the height of your ears and not below, a sea that turns with the view, a
    sound hard in one ear, a sound that lags a fast turn, a click or a cut, a
    loop that stops, or a cue that is too loud.

## Play as normal and send the log

The mod writes `bwvr.log` beside `runblack.exe`. After a play, send it and
each `bwvr-old-*.log` of that day. When an item needs a key, an agent sets
the key in `bwvr.ini` before the play. "Needs a log line" is work for the
agents: the log does not tell the item yet.

- **A crash** (B38). Log: `address space:`, and the last lines before the
  stop.
- **White or invisible story people with Vulkan** (B38): the Lost Brother and
  the Lost Sister of Land 1, and Sable at the cow stone. Log: `vk scene     :
  a texture with no new image`.
- **A grip that opens during a bulk take** (P21). Log: `the grip opens during
  a bulk take`.
- **An old picture in one eye** (B16). Log: `eye clean    : an eye pass drew
  no sky` names the scene of that frame.
- **A lightning in one eye only** (B18). Log: `land light   :`. A flash with
  no such line is not a light on the land.
- **Black frames** in the guide of the first visit of the Temple (B13), and
  with the Vulkan session on the renderer Direct3D 9. Key: `eye_test_luma=1`.
  Log: `eye test     :`.
- **Slow frames and stutter** (N1). Log: `worst frame  :`, `runtime calls:`
  and `slow frame   :`.
- **The pace of the runtime at 120 Hz** (N1). Log: `display      : the
  refresh rate is`.
- **The walk with the stick at 120 Hz** (B27). Log: `Of N frames of the
  headset, M came more than one refresh`, at the end of each walk.
- **The cost of a key in a headset** (N1). The defaults of `frame_send` (also
  with the subtitle card), `render_thread`, `one_walk` and `d3d9_backend`, the
  Vulkan session on the renderer Direct3D 9, `skip_desktop_present=0`, the
  margin around the view, the overlay of the hands and the intro in stereo.
  Key: one of these for a play, with `profile=1`. Log: `frame cost   :`,
  `frame age    :` and `worst frame  :`.
- **The load of a saved game** (N1). Log: `read ahead   :`, `hold         :`
  and `slow frame   :`.
- **The start of the session** (B21), also with a headset that connects
  late. Log: `vr start     : try N found the headset`. For the fall back, key:
  `vr_start_test_fail_session=1`. Log: `the start through the thread of the
  tries failed at`.
- **The skip of the logos.** Log: `intro skip   : a press of the right
  trigger skips the logo screens, at frame N of the intro` shows how early a
  press works.
- **The end at Quit.** Log: the last line is `shutdown     : the mod is shut
  down`.
- **The game window behind.** Log: `the game window is behind`.
- **The menu button of the left controller** (P13). Log: `controllers  : the
  menu button sends Esc`, or `the menu button is dropped`. A press with no
  such line is a press that Virtual Desktop kept.
- **The menu of Virtual Desktop with the trigger held.** Log: `openxr:
  session state` gives each change of the focus. Needs a log line: a button
  that is down when the focus comes back, and the frame at which the mod
  lets the game have it again.
- **A controller after a long cut scene** (B24). Needs a log line: each
  change of the active state of a controller that the runtime gives, with
  the frame.
- **The steps of the tutorial** (B26). Needs a log line: each step of the
  tutorial that starts and ends, with its name, and a step that waits for
  more than 2 minutes.
- **A quest that waits for the player**, for example the Island Keeper of the
  Gods' Playground and the mother of `FollowUs`. Log: `scripts      :` gives
  each question of a script about the player. Needs a log line: a script
  that asks the same question for more than 60 s and gets the same answer.
- **A fight of Creatures** (B32). Log: `hand fight   :` and `fight marks  :`.
- **The land point of a command.** Log: the line of a press says `The arc of
  the press meets`, not `has no land point`.
- **The sound stream.** Log: `audio mixer  : QMixer writes the stream`, with
  "passed a write 0 times".
- **The lines that the player knows.** Log: `hint card    : hint_learn=1: on
  N frames a line that the`.

## The miracles of the game

This section says what each miracle does in the plain game, so that a
player and a desktop run can compare the mod with it. The owner checks one
miracle (check 20, Water on a fire). Each line "To prove" is for a desktop
run (`roadmap.md`, R12): the effect is in the game state or in the eye
pictures.

**The sources.** The game's own text (the help text, the tooltips, the tips
of the loading screen) is in `Scripts\InfoScript2.txt` of the game folder.
The costs and the gestures are in `Scripts\info.dat`. The words here are a
short summary of that text. Where the game files do not say a thing, this
section says "not confirmed".

**Prayer power.** Each miracle costs prayer power from the Worship Site.
The worshippers of the village make it. The costs below are the cost field
of `info.dat`. Desktop runs confirm it for Water (5000), Food (7000), Heal
(6000) and Forest (13000).

**The gestures.** A miracle of the god starts with the spiral. A Creature
miracle starts with the inverse spiral. Then comes the gesture of the
miracle. The names of the shapes below come from the order of the game's
list of gesture types. The spell book and the row over the palm show the
real picture of each one.

**No shower.** The game has no shower or mega version of a miracle. A
stronger version is a power up: draw its gesture (the spiral or the inverse
spiral) while the miracle is in the Hand. Only a miracle from a Worship Site
can power up. The announcer calls the steps "Increase", "Extreme" and
"Maximus". "Shower of Life" is only the name of the Water miracle.

**How to get a miracle in the mod** (`miracles.md`, `village.md`):

1. The spell book: press X (or turn the palm of that hand up), put the
   pointing hand on a miracle, close the grip, and pull 10 cm.
2. A gesture in the air: hold B and draw the spiral and then the gesture of
   the miracle.
3. The Worship Site: tap the grip on the icon of the miracle.
4. A one-off bubble on the land: a short grip on it makes it active. Or
   carry it, and press the trigger.

**How to cast it in the mod** (`miracles.md`, "The grip casts"):

| Kind | Miracles | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At a place | Heal, Forest, Teleport, Explosion | Close the grip with the Hand over the place |
| A pour | Water, Food, Wood, Lightning | Keep the grip closed over the place. Open it to stop |
| A throw | Fireball, the two flocks | Close the grip, swing the hand, and open it |
| A circle | Storm, the two shields | Close the grip and draw a circle over the land. The cast comes when the circle closes |
| On a Creature | The Creature miracles | Close the grip with the gold halo on a Creature (at the Hand, or in the aim) |

Take a miracle out: tap B, or shake the hand. A miracle from a Worship Site
gives its prayer power back. A one-off miracle that you take out is lost.

**What the desktop runs show now.** A desktop run shows that the game gets
the cast (the log line `the game casts ...`). The effect in the world over
time, for example a field that grows, is not proven yet.

**Which miracles you have.** The towns of each land give their miracles.
The debug switch "All miracles" (debug page, Creature) gives each town all
miracles with no cost. But a Worship Site shows only the first 6 that get a
slot, for example Storm, Forest, Fireball, Food and the two shields. Then
Water and Heal do not show.

### Water ("Shower of Life")

- **Plain game.** It makes a flow of water where the Hand is, for as long as
  you hold the cast. The game's tips give three uses:
  - It puts out a fire.
  - It waters a field, so that the crops grow faster.
  - It waters a forest, so that the forest grows larger and gives more wood.
  
  The Creature can learn it and water fields, trees and fires. Not
  confirmed: what it does to a villager or to the Creature (no game file says
  it does anything).
- **How to see that it worked.** The fire test is the clear one: set a house
  on fire with a Fireball, and pour Water on it. The fire must go out. The
  growth of a field or a forest is slow, and the game gives no message for
  it. Not confirmed: how fast the growth is, and whether a field shows a
  change at once.
- **Cost.** 5000. The power up "Water Miracle Increase" (spiral): 7000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the W shape. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the place, and open it to stop.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour, the request from the spell book, and
  a power up from a Worship Site.
- **The owner's check.** Check 20 of "What the owner checks".
- **To prove.** Water on a fire puts it out. Water on a field and on a forest
  changes their growth.

### Food ("Horn of Plenty")

- **Plain game.** It makes food, which drops on the land under the Hand while
  you hold the cast. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** Food lies on the land under the Hand. Not
  confirmed: the look of the pile, and whether the villagers carry it to the
  Village Store by themselves.
- **Cost.** 7000. The power up (spiral): 10000. Not confirmed: what the power
  up makes. One tooltip calls it "Food Miracle Extreme". The help text and
  another tooltip call it "Poisoned Food", food that makes people sick.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the S shape. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the place.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour, the request from the spell book, and a
  power up.
- **To prove.** Food poured near a village lies on the land under the Hand.
  Food with a power up: say what it makes (more food, or food that makes
  people sick).

### Wood ("Structure Elemental")

- **Plain game.** It makes wood on the land under the Hand while you hold the
  cast. The villagers use wood to build. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** Wood lies on the land, and you can take it
  to the Village Store or to a scaffold.
- **Cost.** 7000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the three. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the place.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour.
- **To prove.** Wood poured on the land goes to the Village Store with the
  Hand.

### Forest ("Seeds of Existence")

- **Plain game.** It puts a forest at the place that you choose. The trees
  give wood to the foresters. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** New trees stand at the place.
- **Cost.** 13000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the Cyrillic L. At a place.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip with the Hand over the place.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the cast at a place.
- **A fault of the mod.** Testers find that the camera turns in a circle at
  the Forest miracle (B39). No game file says that the plain game turns the
  camera there.
- **To prove.** Trees stand at the place under the Hand.

### Heal ("Wellness")

- **Plain game.** It heals people. The power up heals all the people of an
  area. The plague quest of the game uses it. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** A sick or hurt villager recovers. In the
  plague quest the good spirit says when you heal one. Not confirmed: a
  picture that shows the heal on a villager.
- **Cost.** 6000. The power up (spiral): 9000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the heart. At a place: the game casts it at a
  place on the land, not on one villager.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip with the Hand over the people.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the cast at a place, and the request from the
  spell book.
- **To prove.** Sick villagers under the Hand recover (for example in the
  plague quest).

### Fireball ("Sphere Aflame")

- **Plain game.** A ball of fire that you throw. The first power up is a
  multi-Fireball, and the second is the largest Fireball. The Creature can
  learn it. The help text says only how to throw it. That it sets the place
  where it lands on fire is known from play, not from a game file.
- **How to see that it worked.** The ball flies from the hand, and the place
  where it lands burns.
- **Cost.** 3500. The power ups (inverse spiral, then spiral): 7000 and
  10000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the vertical line. A throw.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip, swing the hand, and open the grip. It flies
  with the speed of the hand.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the throw with a step of the hand, not with a
  real swing.
- **To prove.** A house that a Fireball hits burns.

### Lightning ("Forked Fire")

- **Plain game.** A bolt of lightning at the target, for as long as you hold
  the cast. The power ups give a larger, more forked bolt. The Creature can
  learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** The bolt starts at the tip of the index
  finger and hits the place under the Hand. The land goes white in both eyes
  (B18). Not confirmed: what the bolt does to each kind of target.
- **Cost.** 5000. The power ups (inverse spiral, then spiral): 7500 and
  10000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. A pour.
- **In the mod.** Keep the grip closed over the target.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the pour.
- **To prove.** The bolt starts at the tip of the index finger and hits the
  place under the Hand, in both eyes.

### Storm ("Storm of Ages")

- **Plain game.** A storm over a circle that you draw. The plain storm is
  wind. The first power up adds rain, and the second adds rain and
  lightning. A storm puts out fires. The game also has a help text for a
  tornado. The Creature can learn the wind, the rain storm, the lightning
  storm and the tornado. Not confirmed: how a player makes a tornado, what
  damage a storm does, and whether its rain waters crops.
- **How to see that it worked.** Wind, then rain and lightning with the power
  ups, over the circle. A fire in the circle goes out.
- **Cost.** 8000. The power ups (inverse spiral, then spiral): 15000 and
  20000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. It needs its own circle.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip and draw a circle over the land. The cast
  comes when the game matches the circle.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the circle and the cast.
- **To prove.** The storm stands over the circle.

### Spiritual Shield ("Sanctuary")

- **Plain game.** A shield over a circle that you draw. Miracles cannot go
  in, but solid objects pass through. The shield takes damage when it is
  attacked, and it uses prayer power for as long as it stands. People under
  a shield cannot be impressed. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** A dome stands over the circle. A miracle
  cast at it does not get in.
- **Cost.** 7000, and more for each second that it stands. No power up in
  `info.dat`, although a help text names a larger shield.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. It needs its own circle.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip and draw a circle over the land.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the circle and the cast.
- **To prove.** The dome stands over the circle, and a miracle cast at it
  does not get in.

### Physical Shield ("Armor of Faith")

- **Plain game.** A shield over a circle that you draw. It stops rocks and
  other solid objects, but miracles go through. It uses prayer power for as
  long as it stands. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** A dome stands over the circle. A rock that
  you throw at it stops.
- **Cost.** 7000, and more for each second that it stands. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then a fork. It needs its own circle.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip and draw a circle over the land.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: only the refusal with no circle ("needs a circle
  on the land first"). Not run: a cast.
- **To prove.** The dome stands over the circle, and a rock thrown at it
  stops.

### Teleport ("Instant Journey")

- **Plain game.** You put two teleport sites on the land. Villagers then go
  from one to the other at once. A tip of the game says to put them at the
  Village Store and the Worship Site, a forest or the fields. The Creature
  can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** Two sites stand on the land, and villagers
  go in at one and come out at the other.
- **Cost.** 5000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the house. At a place, two times.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip with the Hand over the place. Not confirmed:
  whether the second site needs a second cast.
- **Proved.** Not run (N6).
- **To prove.** The two sites stand on the land, and villagers go in at one
  and come out at the other.

### Explosion ("Blast Extremis", the tooltip "Mega-Blast")

- **Plain game.** A large blast at the place that you aim at. The power ups
  make it larger. Only Land 5 gives it. The Creature can learn it.
- **How to see that it worked.** A blast at the place. Not confirmed: what
  the blast breaks.
- **Cost.** 16000. The power ups (inverse spiral, then spiral): 32000 and
  60000.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the star. At a place.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip with the Hand over the place.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the cast at a place.
- **To prove.** The blast is at the place under the Hand.

### Flying Flock ("Sky Full", the tooltip "Winged Creatures")

- **Plain game.** A large flock of flying creatures that impresses people.
  The game gives it only as a reward. Not confirmed: whether a good god and
  an evil god get different birds.
- **How to see that it worked.** A flock flies from the hand.
- **Cost.** 12000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the square spiral. A throw.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip, swing the hand, and open it.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the throw.
- **To prove.** The birds fly where the throw goes.

### Ground Flock ("Beasts of Earth", the tooltip "Wolves")

- **Plain game.** A pack of dangerous wolves on the land. The game gives it
  only as a reward.
- **How to see that it worked.** Wolves run on the land where the throw goes.
- **Cost.** 14000. No power up.
- **Gesture.** Spiral, then the inverse square spiral. A throw.
- **In the mod.** Close the grip, swing the hand, and open it.
- **Proved.** Desktop runs: the throw.
- **To prove.** The wolves come down where the throw goes.

### The Creature miracles

Each one acts on the target Creature, your own or a different one, for a
time. The narrator says when it "has worn off". Not confirmed: how long each
one lasts. Each is a cast on a Creature: close the grip with the gold halo
on the Creature. The gesture is the inverse spiral and then the shape below.

| Miracle | What it does to the target Creature | Cost | Gesture after the inverse spiral |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Freeze | It freezes it | 8000 | The star |
| Shrink | It makes it small | 6000 | A fork |
| Enlarge | It makes it large | 7000 | A fork |
| Weaken | It makes it weak | 8000 | A fork |
| Strengthen | It makes it strong | 8000 | A fork |
| Invisibility | It makes it invisible | 9000 | The infinity |
| Compassion ("Loving") | It makes it friendly and loving | 6000 | The heart |
| Anger ("Aggressive") | It makes it furious | 6000 | The vertical line |
| Itchy ("Holy Flies") | It irritates it | 8000 | The reverse S |

- **Proved.** Desktop runs: Strengthen and Freeze on a Creature. Not run:
  the other seven (N6).
- **To prove.** Freeze stops the Creature, and the narrator later says that
  the miracle has worn off. Shrink, Enlarge, Invisibility, Weaken,

**Miracles that a player does not cast.** The game has seven more Creature
miracles: Fat, Thin, Hungry, Frightened, Tired, Ill and Thirsty. Each has a
record of a seed and a line "Your Creature has learned how to cast ...".
They have no gesture, and their records of magic have the name NONE. So a
player cannot ask for one. Not confirmed: whether a Creature or a script can
cast one in v1.42.

**The Creature casts miracles too.** The Creature can learn each miracle of
the god above, and the game says when it learns one. A tip of the game says
that a Creature casts a miracle at once when you give it the miracle.
